# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit other people's findings and work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@keenanlk](https://github.com/keenanlk), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code. They may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.
